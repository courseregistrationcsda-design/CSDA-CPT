Using Guide Search and reviewed online references

Open Admin → Governance → General Guidelines. Guide Search understands ordinary task descriptions, casual English, common abbreviations, partial phrases, and selected misspellings. It searches the local guide rather than requiring exact policy wording.

Examples of questions

  • “I cannot log in”
  • “Why is clearance pending?”
  • “Student paid too much”
  • “How do I find an old record?”
  • “Receipt does not match”
  • “Certificate has the wrong name”
  • “What does not yet competent mean?”
  • “How do I restore a backup?”

How matching works

  1. The app simplifies punctuation and casual wording.
  2. It recognizes related operational words, abbreviations, word fragments, and small spelling errors.
  3. It ranks the closest guide topics and returns exact steps plus common problems and solutions.
  4. If no close match appears, use fewer words or one of these task words: payment, receipt, enrollment, trainer, audit, refund, certificate, backup, archive, password, or offline.
Privacy: The typed question is processed locally and is never sent to a web search provider. Do not type learner names, payment references, receipt details, passwords, recovery keys, or other personal information into help searches.

When internet is available

Relevant topics may show reviewed links to official resources such as TESDA, the Philippine National Privacy Commission, the Official Gazette, or Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas. Opening a link is optional and leaves the app. The typed Guide Search question is not attached to the link or transmitted. External resources provide current regulatory or language context; the app’s record-specific validation and CSDA-approved procedure remain authoritative for operating the toolkit.

When offline

All local guide answers, steps, troubleshooting, and process explanations remain available. Only the external reference pages require a connection.

1. Access, roles, and shift control

Opening a session

  1. Use an approved CSDA device and browser profile.
  2. Enter your own full name at every Admin sign-in. Confirm the displayed identity before changing a record.
  3. Do not share the current Administrator password or place it in printed guides.
  4. During credential setup, store the one-time recovery key in CSDA’s approved offline credential record, separate from app backups. The app does not retain the clear key.
  5. If recovery is used, securely replace the stored key because the old key is invalidated and a new one is shown once.
  6. Begin in Today / Work Queue. Use the Admin sidebar groups—Daily Work, Catalogue, Operations, and Governance—to reach the correct workspace. Class Monitor opens in its own operational window.
  7. At shift opening, review pending payment verifications, unresolved Done audits from oldest to newest, and recently invalidated clearances.
  8. Confirm that the most recent protected backup is appropriate for the current volume and risk of work.
Stop and escalate: If the displayed staff identity is wrong, the database appears unexpectedly empty, an import was not authorized, or records differ materially from their supporting documents, stop editing and notify authorized management.

Role attribution under the shared Admin login

The app uses named role attribution rather than separate role accounts. The person performing an action must be identified honestly. Trainer and academic names come from the enrollment record; final approval records the active Administrator.

2. Enrollment intake and quality control

Illustration 1. The controlled record lifecycle. Evidence must be saved at each stage.

Learner identity and age path

  • Legal name spelling and order match the learner’s supporting information.
  • Birth date is correct and produces the correct minor/adult path.
  • Email, phone, and address are current.
  • Minor: parent/legal guardian and guarantor details are complete.
  • Adult: Emergency Contact is used when contact information is provided.

Course, price, and schedule

  • Correct course or course bundle is selected.
  • Dates, times, instructional hours, and delivery expectations are reviewed.
  • Assigned trainer is selected from the enrollment form.
  • Discount is eligible for the course type. Short courses and workshops may use only Group of 3+ or Early Bird discounts.
  • Totals are reviewed before payment expectations are communicated.

Finalization

  1. Move through all six steps and resolve validation messages.
  2. Use Finalize and create PDF.
  3. Compare the PDF against the saved record before releasing it.
  4. If incorrect, return to the form and correct the source record; do not edit the PDF externally.

3. Payment and receipt verification

Required transaction data

Every payment must include date, amount, method, reference/transaction number, notes, and a readable receipt image. On a supported phone or tablet, the receipt upload control may open the rear camera directly; staff may instead choose an existing image. Review framing and readability before saving.

CheckAccept whenReject or hold when

  1. Open the receipt and transaction data.
  2. Check the actual GCash, bank, or merchant portal. A screenshot is supporting evidence, not independent confirmation.
  3. Compare amount, date, payer, and reference.
  4. Approve only when reconciled. Otherwise keep pending or reject with a clear operational note.
Clearance effect: Financial clearance requires enough approved payments and a receipt attached to every approved payment.

4. Class monitoring and enrollment changes

The timeline consists of Scheduled, Ongoing, Almost Done, and Done. Almost Done means one or two sessions remain.

  1. Use automatic schedule status unless an authorized correction is necessary.
  2. A manual timeline move changes schedule state only. It does not alter payment, academic, trainer, or final Admin approval.
  3. When a record enters Done, open the audit immediately or process the oldest unresolved Done record first.
  4. Use Open and edit full enrollment for corrections.
  5. After saving, review the official timestamped change entry, recomputed total, course list, schedule, and hours.
Material post-clearance edit: The previous clearance becomes stale and is invalidated. Reperform the audit before issuing corrected documents.

5. Completion Audit

Illustration 2. Independent gates converge at final approval. One completed gate never substitutes for another.

Record-specific pending list

Read every item under Certification Clearance Pending. It identifies missing evidence and gives the action needed for the open record. A disabled approval button means at least one gate remains unresolved.

Financial gate

  • Enrollment balance is fully covered by approved payments.
  • Every approved payment has a receipt.
  • Any overpayment refund is issued, documented, and confirmed.

Academic gate

  1. Enter a working portfolio/archive link.
  2. Open and review the evidence.
  3. Press Confirm portfolio verified. The academic sign-off name is automatically taken from the learner’s enrollment.

Trainer gate

  1. Enter the official completion/performance report.
  2. Record either COMPETENT or NOT YET COMPETENT.
  3. Resolve attendance.
  4. Press Confirm trainer report verified. The name comes from the assigned trainer.
Competency principle: A result must be recorded, but it is the trainer’s professional assessment—not an administrative passing score. NOT YET COMPETENT does not itself block clearance.

Status and progress

The enrollment must be Active. Dropped or Cancelled records are document-ineligible. Use Save audit progress when stopping before approval. If confirmed evidence changes, review and confirm that section again.

6. Refund reconciliation

Illustration 3. Refund confirmation occurs only after the money is returned and evidence is verified.
  1. Use the automatically calculated overpayment amount.
  2. Issue the refund through an authorized external channel.
  3. Enter the exact amount and reference.
  4. Upload the refund receipt.
  5. Independently match the receipt and transfer, then press Confirm refund completed.

7. Final approval and controlled document release

  1. Read the pending list again and independently review all supporting records.
  2. Enter the current Final Admin password only after the review.
  3. Select Approve clearance and generate documents.
  4. Compare the Clearance Form and Certificate previews with the source record: learner, course, hours, trainer, approving Administrator, and date.
  5. Use each document’s separate Print / Save action.
  6. Distribute only to authorized recipients through an approved channel.
Correction rule: Never fix an issued PDF using an external editor. Correct the source enrollment or audit, regenerate, re-review, and reissue.

Trainer time and generated calendar assignments

When multiple individual trainers are assigned, instructional time is divided equally: two trainers receive 50/50, three receive approximately one-third each, and so on. The generated calendar proposes sequential session blocks and highlights where each selected course ends. Admin may choose a different trainer on any generated session and may manually adjust assignment dates and times. Treat the suggested next date as guidance; resolve availability, holidays, and clashes before finalizing.

8. Backup, import, and recovery

Illustration 4. Always preview recognized contents before choosing an import mode.
  1. Create password-protected full-session CSV ZIP backups at regular checkpoints. Verify the import preview reports successful manifest integrity before Replace or Merge.
  2. Store backup files and passwords separately.
  3. Before import, note current record counts and create a fresh backup when possible.
  4. Preview recognized contents. Choose Replace only for deliberate restoration; choose Merge when retaining local data.
  5. After import, verify a sample of enrollments, payments, receipts, schedules, trainers, audit history, and artwork.
  6. To remove the app and local data, use the guarded removal control. Enter the Administrator password, allow the mandatory full backup ZIP to download, confirm safe custody of the ZIP and password, then authorize local clearing. Finally use the browser or device menu to uninstall the PWA itself.

9. Privacy, exceptions, and incidents

Privacy

  • Access only records needed for assigned duties.
  • Do not copy learner, guardian, receipt, or payment data to personal devices.
  • Use approved distribution channels and recipients.
  • Close Admin access on shared devices and avoid leaving records visible.

Policy exceptions

Never invent evidence or bypass a gate. Escalate genuine exceptions to authorized CSDA management and preserve the decision, authorizer, date, reason, and affected record in official notes.

Incident response

  1. Stop the action that could worsen the issue.
  2. Preserve the current record and relevant audit history.
  3. Record what happened, when, who observed it, and which records may be affected.
  4. Notify authorized management or the designated privacy/security contact.
  5. Do not delete evidence or restore a backup until authorized.

10. Quick checklists

Before final clearance

  • Enrollment is Active.
  • Payments cover the net total.
  • Every approved payment has a receipt.
  • Refund is issued and confirmed when applicable.
  • Portfolio link works and is confirmed.
  • Trainer report is complete.
  • Either competency result is recorded.
  • Attendance is resolved.
  • Pending list has no unresolved gate.
  • Administrator independently reviewed the record.

End of shift

  • Active work is saved.
  • Pending payments and audits are identified for follow-up.
  • Protected backup is created when required.
  • Exports and printed documents are secured.
  • Admin access is closed on shared devices.
