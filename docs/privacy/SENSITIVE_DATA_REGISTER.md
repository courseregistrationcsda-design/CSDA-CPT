# CSDA Toolkit — Sensitive Data Register

This register documents why sensitive fields exist and the retention decision that must be applied by CSDA's authorized operational owner.

| Data | Operational/legal purpose | Minimum-access rule | Retention rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Student full name | Identify enrollment, schedule, billing, and issued forms | Enrollment/administrative staff and assigned trainer when necessary | Approved student/enrollment record period |
| Date of birth | Determine whether parent/legal-guardian consent is required | Enrollment/authorized administrative staff | Approved enrollment/consent period; remove when no longer required |
| Educational attainment | Course suitability and enrollment record | Enrollment and authorized academic staff | Approved student-record period |
| Provider/school | Coordination and enrollment context | Enrollment and authorized academic staff | Approved enrollment period |
| Emergency contact | Reach a responsible person for an adult student or emergency | Authorized enrollment/operations staff | Active enrollment plus approved follow-up period |
| Parent/legal guardian details | Verify authority and contact the responsible adult for a minor | Authorized enrollment/administrative staff | Approved minor-consent and enrollment period |
| Home address | Enrollment/contact record where operationally required | Authorized administrative staff only | Do not collect if unnecessary; otherwise approved enrollment period |
| Consent declarations and signature timestamp | Evidence that required notices and terms were accepted | Authorized administrative/legal staff | Approved legal/consent retention period |
| Trainer details | Assignment and handoff of necessary enrollment information | Enrollment and authorized academic staff | Assignment/employment operational period |
| Payment amount/reference/method | Reconciliation and accounting | Authorized finance/administrative staff | Approved accounting/tax retention period |
| Receipt images | Manual payment verification | Authorized finance verifiers only | Delete or securely archive promptly after verification under finance policy |
| Facility user, organization, purpose, and notes | Operate and bill Shared Service Facility sessions | Authorized facility/finance staff | Approved facility/accounting period |
| Local activity trail | Operational troubleshooting and accountability convenience | Authorized administrators | Short operational period; never treat as tamper-proof evidence |
| Exported backup | Recovery of all included records | Named authorized custodians only | Rotate and securely delete according to the longest included approved period |

## Collection rule

Collect only what is needed for the stated purpose. Optional fields should remain blank when CSDA has no operational or legal reason to collect them. Do not enter passwords, government identifiers, medical details, unrelated personal notes, or full banking credentials into free-text fields.

## Governance decisions still owned by CSDA

The application cannot determine legal retention periods. CSDA must approve exact durations, the authorized roles for each category, the backup location, deletion authority, and incident-reporting contact.
