# CSDA Toolkit — Local Data Retention and Privacy Guide

## Where records are stored

The toolkit has no application server. Catalogue settings, enrollment records, contact details, consent records, payment references, verification status, facility sessions, and the local activity trail are stored in the current browser profile using local storage.

Records do not automatically follow a staff member to another computer, browser, browser profile, or private/incognito session.

## Who can access them

Anyone who can open the unlocked device and its browser profile may be able to read or copy locally stored records. The administrator password is a workflow gate for ordinary screens; it is not encryption and cannot protect records from someone with device access or browser developer tools.

Required safeguards:

1. Use an approved CSDA-managed device account.
2. Lock the device whenever it is unattended.
3. Do not share the operating-system or browser-profile password.
4. Do not use a public/shared browser profile.
5. Keep the browser and operating system updated.
6. Store exported backups only in an approved encrypted or access-controlled location.

## Retention

Operational owners must apply CSDA's approved retention schedule. Until a formal schedule is supplied, the toolkit must not be treated as authority to keep personal information indefinitely.

Recommended working rules pending formal approval:

- Unfinished/test records: remove immediately after testing or abandonment.
- Receipt images: remove or archive under the approved accounting process immediately after verification; do not keep copies in personal Downloads folders.
- Enrollment/contact/consent records: retain only for the approved enrollment, legal, and student-record period.
- Facility sessions: retain only for the approved operational/accounting period.
- Local activity trail: convenience only; not tamper-proof legal evidence.
- Backups: rotate and securely delete superseded copies according to the same underlying record-retention rules.

## Data loss

Clearing site data, resetting the browser profile, losing/replacing the device, browser corruption, or opening a different browser can make the local records unavailable. The toolkit cannot recover them from a server.

Use **Admin → CSV & Backup → Download full backup**. The app records the most recent successful export time and warns when there is no recorded backup, at least ten saved changes have occurred, or the recorded backup is at least seven days old.

## Restoring

Paste the contents of a toolkit backup into the Restore box and select **Restore from backup**. Confirm the restored catalogue, enrollments, payments, and facility data before disposing of the previous device or backup.

## Incident handling

If a device or backup containing personal data is lost, accessed by an unauthorized person, or sent to the wrong recipient, stop using the affected copy and notify CSDA's designated privacy/security contact immediately. Do not attempt to conceal or privately resolve a suspected breach.
